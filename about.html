About

I'm Vishnu — a BSc (Hons) Computing Systems graduate from Ulster University London. I'm aiming at solutions and implementation engineering for AI products: I build the thing, put it in front of real users, and explain how it works to people who don't care about the code. My stack is Python and TypeScript, REST and LLM APIs, and Azure.

LightGuide is the project I'd start a conversation with. It's an AI lighting assistant: upload a reference photo and get a lighting diagram and a step-by-step plan to recreate it, labelled honestly as estimates to confirm with a test shot. I opened it to working photographers for feedback, and built an Arduino sensor prototype (86.5% accuracy on a held-out session) to measure light positions automatically.

The pattern across my projects is the same instinct: make the system explainable. A tax assistant where a rule engine computes and the LLM only explains. A misinformation tracker that matches claims semantically instead of by keyword. A file-transfer platform that gets its insight from metadata so it never has to read your files.

Seven original public repos, three deployments live right now. If any of it comes up in an interview, I can open the repo and walk through it.
