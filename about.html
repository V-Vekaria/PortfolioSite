About

Practical by habit, curious on purpose.

The short version

I'm Vishnu — a BSc (Hons) Computing Systems graduate from Ulster University London. I'm aiming at solutions engineering for AI products: I build the thing, put it in front of real users, and explain how it works to people who don't care about the code. My stack is Python and TypeScript, REST and LLM APIs, and Azure.

LightGuide is the project I'd start a conversation with. It's an AI lighting assistant: upload a reference photo and get a lighting diagram and a step-by-step plan to recreate it, labelled honestly as estimates to confirm with a test shot. I opened it to working photographers for feedback, and I built an Arduino sensor prototype (86.5% accuracy on a held-out session) to measure light positions automatically.

The pattern across my projects is the same instinct: make the system explainable. A tax assistant where a rule engine computes and the LLM only explains. A misinformation tracker that matches claims semantically instead of by keyword. A file-transfer platform that gets its insight from metadata so it never has to read your files.

Outside the repos

Alongside the degree I worked at Sainsbury's — September 2024 to April 2026 — where I coordinated teams of five to eight colleagues during peak trading. Since July 2026 I've worked at Mepani News in Kingsbury, advising customers on money transfers and talking suppliers through deals. Both taught me to explain things plainly to people in a hurry.

I've also done freelance client work: a portfolio site for a London photographer, taken from brief through drafts and feedback to launch, and a redesign concept for SKSST Temple focused on clearer navigation while keeping their community content.
