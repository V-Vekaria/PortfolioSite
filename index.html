About

I'm a Computing Systems graduate from Ulster University London who takes AI products from idea to real users. Most recently that's LightGuide, an AI lighting assistant I opened to working photographers for testing, and Longhand, a deployed tax assistant built at the AMD Developer Hackathon.

I'm happiest between the product and the customer: demos, discovery, APIs and getting something working in someone else's hands. Day to day that means Python, TypeScript, REST and LLM APIs, and Azure.

Alongside the degree I've worked customer-facing jobs, which taught me to explain things plainly to people in a hurry. I'm open to full-time solutions and implementation roles in London or remote — get in touch.
